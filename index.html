TxODDS · 2024 – Present

Scores-DQ: Sports Data Quality Platform

Sole developer until May 2026, then team lead

Production platform that validates live sports play-by-play data against ESPN and StatBroadcast. It began as a self-initiated QA tool and is now the data quality product I lead at TxODDS.

  • Built and deployed to production in 2024, cutting weekly recheck labor from 96 to 12 hours
  • Scaled it to recheck 80% more fixtures season-over-season
  • Delivered ~20 client-driven quality initiatives, cutting score adjustments 63% and player-stat adjustments 64%
  • Re-architected into a FastAPI backend and Shiny frontend on Kubernetes, then led the migration of its computation onto scheduled Snowflake marts
  • Python
  • FastAPI
  • Shiny
  • Snowflake
  • dbt
  • Airflow

Loyola University Chicago · Fall 2021

Milwaukee Assessor's Office: Property Comparables

Consulting capstone, team of 3

Consulting capstone for the City of Milwaukee Assessor's Office. Worked from the Office's dataset of 122,096 residential properties to find comparable properties that help the Office defend appraisal values on appeal.

  • Built a K-D tree nearest-neighbor search in scikit-learn that retrieves comparable sold properties using 13 property features
  • Ranked price drivers with a gradient boosted model; neighborhood code led (57.6 relative influence), ahead of quality grade (22.0)
  • Cut missing half-bath ratings from 93,935 to 35 by confirming with the client that blanks meant zero half baths
  • R
  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • Gradient boosting
  • EDA

About Me

Why data science? When I was 8, I spent every Sunday night trying to answer the same question: "Why do the Chicago Bears always lose?" Almost twenty years later I still can't answer that question, but at least adversity builds resilience... right?

In 2022, I graduated Magna Cum Laude from Loyola University Chicago and was awarded Departmental Honors in Data Science. Despite the lockdown and academic instability, I persevered and spent my time taking on projects in R, machine learning, and data science consulting.

I'm a Data Quality Analyst Team Lead at TxODDS in Chicago. I joined in 2024 in one of the first batches of hires at the new Chicago branch, became its first quality assurance hire and built the QA function there, then built the automation platform that became Scores-DQ. Today I own the Snowflake, dbt, and Airflow pipeline behind Scores-DQ, lead the team I built through a hiring process I designed, and contribute to commercial data products for TxODDS's London data team. My work has spanned sports, healthcare receivables, and public health data.

In my free time, I love building custom keyboards, camping & fishing, playing guitar and piano, and thrifting! If you want to own your first custom keyboard, connect with me here!
